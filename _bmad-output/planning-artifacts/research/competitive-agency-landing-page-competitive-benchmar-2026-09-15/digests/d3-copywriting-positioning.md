# Digest: Copywriting, Value Propositions & Trust Signals

## 1. Hook Formulations & Positioning
- **The Scale & Impact Hook:** "We design & build websites that convert visitors into loyal customers."
- **The Speed & Quality Hook:** "High-velocity web development without compromising on bespoke design and clean architecture."
- **The Engineering-First Hook:** "Bridging the gap between award-winning creative design and production-grade engineering."

## 2. Microcopy & De-Risking Strategy
- Guarantees & Milestones: "Milestone-based delivery", "100% code ownership", "Dedicated project Slack channel".
- Budget Microcopy: "Sharing your budget helps us recommend the right architecture and timeline without wasting your time."
- Alternative Inbound: Direct partner/founder email for high-profile clients who skip forms.
