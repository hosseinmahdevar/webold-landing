# Digest: Subpage Structure & Conversion Funnels

## 1. Case Study Detail Pages (/work/[slug])
- **Header:** Full-bleed interactive asset, client category, timeline, deliverables list.
- **Executive Summary:** Quick-read summary (Challenge, Solution, Impact stats in a 3-metric KPI bar).
- **The Story Flow:**
  1. Context & Business Challenge (Why old solution failed).
  2. Research & UX Architecture (Wireframes, user flows, architecture diagrams).
  3. UI & Design System (Components, micro-interactions, responsive states).
  4. Engineering & Performance (Tech stack choices, Lighthouse 100 score, load time reduction).
  5. Business Outcome & Client Testimonial.
- **Footer CTA:** "Have a similar project? Let's talk."

## 2. Services Page (/services)
- **Modular Packaging:** From single-sprint MVP to complete enterprise web design & development.
- **Deliverables Matrix:** Clear list of what is included (Figma files, source code, CI/CD setup, SEO optimization, CMS integration).
- **Tech Stack & Standards:** Explicit performance benchmarks (Core Web Vitals, accessibility WCAG AA, TypeScript, Next.js).

## 3. Contact & Lead Qualification (/contact)
- **Component Pattern:** Radio Chips / Button Pills for frictionless 1-tap mobile selection.
- **Budget Brackets & Anchoring:**
  - Minimum starting threshold to eliminate bargain hunters without manual screening (e.g., `< $5,000`, `$5k-$15k`, `$15k-$30k`, `$30k+` or "Exploring / Need advice").
- **Funnel Progression:**
  - Step 1: Project Type (Web App, Corporate Landing, E-commerce, Redesign).
  - Step 2: Timeline (Urgent < 1 mo, 1-3 mos, Flexible).
  - Step 3: Budget Range.
  - Step 4: Contact details & project brief description.
