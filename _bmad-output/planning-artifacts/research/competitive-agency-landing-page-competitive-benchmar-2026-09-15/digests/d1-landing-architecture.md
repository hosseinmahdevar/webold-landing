# Digest: Landing Page Architecture & Section Hierarchy

## Core Findings & Teardowns
- **Section 1: The Hero (Above the Fold)**
  - Formula: Bold Category Hook + Interactive/Video Showreel + Immediate Primary CTA.
  - Examples: MetaLab ("We design and build products that millions of people use every day"), BASIC/DEPT ("A global agency making brands culturally relevant").
  - Pattern: Eliminates ambiguity in under 3 seconds; communicates caliber and target tier.
- **Section 2: Credibility Bar / Social Proof Anchor**
  - Monochrome logo cloud or subtle marquee scroll.
  - High-impact anchor metric banner: e.g., "$4.2B+ client funding/exits", "100M+ active users".
- **Section 3: Curated Work / Case Studies**
  - 3-5 high-impact cards with video hover states.
  - Anatomy of card: Client Name & Category + Project Scope + Tangible Impact Metric (+140% conversions, $40M Series A) + Cinematic Device Mockup.
- **Section 4: Capabilities / Services Bento Grid**
  - 3-4 distinct pillars (Product Design, Web Development, Design Systems, Growth Engineering).
  - Explicit tech stack indicators (Next.js, React, Tailwind, Framer Motion).
- **Section 5: Flagship Deep Dive / Narrative Spotlight**
  - Embedding 1 full story into the homepage flow: The Problem -> The Architecture/Design Strategy -> The Execution -> The Measured ROI.
- **Section 6: Client Testimonials & Recognition**
  - C-level/VP quotes with photo, full title, and company logo.
  - Recognition badges (Awwwards, FWA, Webby, GitHub stars).
- **Section 7: High-Intent Pre-Qualifying Intake (CTA)**
  - Multi-step interactive form with budget tiers, project timeline, and direct partner email fallback.
